# Banking Relationship Submission Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist ensures complete and professional submission of banking relationship materials to new banking partners. Use this for commercial banks, custodians, payment processors, and trust companies.

---

## Pre-Submission Preparation

### ☐ 1. Identify Target Banking Partner

**Document the following:**
- [ ] Bank name and jurisdiction
- [ ] Relationship manager contact (if known)
- [ ] Risk/compliance officer contact (if known)
- [ ] Services required (operating accounts, treasury, ACH, wire, etc.)
- [ ] Projected monthly transaction volume
- [ ] Anticipated initial deposit amount

**Note:** If relationship manager is known, warm introduction is always preferable to cold submission.

---

### ☐ 2. Customize Cover Letter

**Required customizations:**
- [ ] Fill in [Bank Name] (appears 2x)
- [ ] Fill in [Relationship Manager Name]
- [ ] Fill in [Current Date]
- [ ] Fill in [Name], [Title], [Contact Information] in signature block
- [ ] Review "What We Are Requesting" section—adjust services list if needed
- [ ] Add letterhead or format on company letterhead

**Optional customizations:**
- [ ] Add specific transaction volume estimates
- [ ] Reference any prior conversations or introductions
- [ ] Add cc: to internal counsel if relationship warrants

---

### ☐ 3. Prepare Supporting Documents

**Required documents (in this order):**
1. [ ] Cover Letter (1 page, customized)
2. [ ] Bank Risk Memo (5 pages, no customization needed)
3. [ ] Executive Summary (from `/docs/ayg-platform/00-executive-summary/README.md`)
4. [ ] Contact and Governance Sheet (see template below)

**Format options:**
- [ ] **PDF packet** (recommended): Combine all documents into single PDF
- [ ] **Separate PDFs**: Each document as separate file in one email
- [ ] **Printed packet**: For in-person submission or mail

## Submission Process

### ☐ 5. Internal Review (Before Sending)

**Required approvals:**
- [ ] Legal counsel review (if applicable)
- [ ] CFO approval
- [ ] CEO sign-off on cover letter

**Quality checks:**
- [ ] All contact information is current
- [ ] No typos or formatting errors
- [ ] All bracketed fields [like this] are filled in
- [ ] Documents are in correct order
- [ ] PDF is readable and properly formatted

---

### ☐ 6. Submit to Banking Partner

**Preferred method:** Email to relationship manager (if known)

**Email template:**

```
Subject: New Account Application – AYG Insurance & Financial Services

[Relationship Manager Name],

[IF WARM INTRO: Thank you for the introduction and opportunity to discuss 
a banking relationship with [Bank Name].]

I am writing to formally request commercial banking services for AYG 
Insurance & Financial Services LLC. We are an employer-sponsored benefits 
platform operating within traditional insurance and ERISA frameworks.

Attached please find:
1. Cover letter outlining our request
2. Risk assessment memo for your compliance team
3. Executive summary of our business
4. Contact and governance information

We are seeking standard commercial banking services (operating accounts, 
ACH, wire services) and anticipate straightforward account activity 
consistent with employer benefits platform operations.

I understand your team will need to complete due diligence, and I am 
available to answer questions or provide additional documentation as needed.

Please confirm receipt and advise on next steps in your review process.

Best regards,

[Name]
[Title]
AYG Insurance & Financial Services LLC
[Contact Information]
```

**Alternative method:** If no relationship manager identified, contact bank's business banking department or commercial accounts team.

---

### ☐ 7. Follow-Up Protocol

**Timeline:**

- [ ] **Day 3:** If no acknowledgment, send follow-up email confirming receipt
- [ ] **Week 1:** If no response, phone call to relationship manager or main business line
- [ ] **Week 2:** If still no response, consider escalating to branch manager or seeking different banking partner

**During review process:**

- [ ] Respond promptly to all information requests (within 24 hours)
- [ ] Keep detailed notes of all conversations
- [ ] Maintain document version control (if any updates requested)
- [ ] Loop in legal counsel on any unexpected questions

## Common Bank Questions & Responses

### Q: "Do you handle cryptocurrency?"

**Response:** "No. We operate a traditional insurance and employee benefits platform. We use blockchain-based reporting tools for internal audit and capital partner transparency, but we do not custody, trade, or process cryptocurrency. All participant funds remain in traditional fiat banking channels."

---

### Q: "Why the memo about digital assets if you don't handle them?"

**Response:** "We've found that proactively addressing potential questions streamlines the review process. The memo clarifies that our use of blockchain technology is limited to read-only reporting infrastructure and does not create any custody, ERISA, or securities exposure for banking partners."

---

### Q: "Are you a FinTech company?"

**Response:** "No, we are a regulated insurance and employee benefits platform. We use modern technology for reporting and operations, but our business model is traditional employer-sponsored benefits administration."

---

### Q: "Who are your regulators?"

**Response:** "We operate under state insurance department oversight [list states], DOL/ERISA for employee benefit plans, and IRS for tax-qualified plans. We maintain all required licenses and operate under formal legal opinions from [counsel name]."

---

### Q: "What's your expected transaction volume?"

**Response:** [Provide specific estimates based on actual business projections]
- Monthly inbound: [$ amount, # of transactions]
- Monthly outbound: [$ amount, # of transactions]
- Average transaction size: [$ amount]
- Source of funds: Employer premium payments, subscription fees

---

### Q: "Can we see your legal opinions?"

**Response:** "Our formal legal opinions are confidential attorney-client privileged documents. However, we can provide a summary letter from counsel confirming ERISA, tax, and regulatory compliance if that would be helpful for your review."

## Post-Approval Actions

### ☐ 8. Account Opening

Once approved:
- [ ] Complete bank's standard account opening forms
- [ ] Provide corporate resolutions (banking authority)
- [ ] Submit any additional KYC/AML documentation required
- [ ] Make initial deposit
- [ ] Set up online banking access
- [ ] Configure ACH/wire templates

---

### ☐ 9. Relationship Maintenance

Ongoing:
- [ ] Annual certification to bank (if required): "No material change to business model"
- [ ] Notify bank of any regulatory license changes
- [ ] Maintain relationship manager contact
- [ ] Monitor for any policy changes affecting account

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

**If bank requests any of the following, consult legal counsel before proceeding:**

- [ ] Acknowledgment that you are a "money services business" or "cryptocurrency business"
- [ ] Enhanced due diligence beyond standard commercial banking
- [ ] Specialized crypto banking terms or conditions
- [ ] Significantly higher fees than standard commercial accounts
- [ ] Restrictions inconsistent with traditional benefits platform operations

**These may indicate misunderstanding of business model—address immediately.**
