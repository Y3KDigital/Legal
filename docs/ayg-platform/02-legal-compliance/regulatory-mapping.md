# Regulatory Mapping & Compliance Framework

## Purpose

This document maps the AYG platform to applicable U.S. regulatory frameworks and demonstrates compliance through program design.

---

## ERISA Compliance

### Applicability

The Self-Insured Concierge Managed Medical Plan is subject to ERISA as an employee welfare benefit plan.

### ERISA Requirements & Compliance

| Requirement | Compliance Mechanism |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Written Plan Document | Formal plan document adopted by employer |
| Summary Plan Description (SPD) | SPD provided to all participants |
| Claims Procedure | Claims and appeals process documented |
| Fiduciary Duties | Employer/sponsor acts as plan fiduciary |
| Reporting (Form 5500) | Filed annually (if required based on plan size) |
| Prohibited Transactions | No self-dealing; assets held for exclusive benefit of participants |

### ERISA Asset Protection

* Plan assets held in trust or by regulated insurance company
* RWAs do not touch or reference plan assets
* Clear separation between plan assets and operating capital

---

## ACA Compliance

### Minimum Essential Coverage (MEC)

The plan provides MEC, satisfying the individual mandate (where applicable).

**Design Features:**

* Coverage for preventive services without cost-sharing
* Coverage provided to employees and dependents
* Reported on Form 1095-C

### Employer Mandate (§4980H)

For applicable large employers (50+ FTEs):

* MEC offered to 95% of full-time employees
* Coverage must be affordable (safe harbor methods available)
* Minimum value requirement met

### Non-Discrimination Rules

* Plan tested annually for compliance with ACA non-discrimination rules
* Highly compensated employees do not receive disproportionate benefits
* Eligibility criteria applied consistently

---

## IRC §125 Cafeteria Plan Compliance

### Requirements

| Requirement | Compliance Mechanism |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Written Plan Document | Cafeteria plan document adopted |
| Qualified Benefits Only | Only §125-eligible benefits offered |
| Election Requirements | Annual elections required; changes only for qualifying events |
| Non-Discrimination Testing | Annual testing performed |
| Benefit Payment | Benefits paid only for eligible expenses |

### Permitted Benefits

* Health insurance premiums (MEC)
* Health FSA contributions
* Other qualified benefits per §125

### Non-Permitted

* Deferred compensation
* Cash bonuses (unless structured as wellness rewards per IRS guidance)

---

## IRC §105 Self-Insured Medical Reimbursement

### Requirements

* Benefits provided under written plan
* Reimbursement only for medical expenses under §213(d)
* Non-discrimination rules for self-insured plans

### Design Compliance

* Plan document specifies covered expenses
* Claims substantiation required
* Benefits available to all eligible employees

---

## Health FSA (IRC §125)

### Requirements

| Requirement | Compliance Mechanism |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Election Before Period Begins | Annual election during open enrollment |
| Use-It-Or-Lose-It | Carryover or grace period option utilized |
| $3,200 Limit (2024) | Plan enforces annual limit |
| Eligible Expenses | Only §213(d) medical expenses reimbursed |
| Substantiation | Claims require documentation |

---

## COBRA Continuation Coverage

### Applicability

Plans covering 20+ employees must offer COBRA.

### Compliance

* Qualifying events trigger continuation rights
* Notice requirements met
* Premium calculations per DOL rules (102% of cost)
* Coverage continues for qualified period

---

## HIPAA Privacy & Security

### PHI Protection

* Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with service providers
* Minimum necessary standard applied
* Notice of Privacy Practices provided
* Security measures implemented

### Breach Notification

* Breach response plan in place
* Notification procedures documented

---

## State Insurance Regulation

### Licensing

Where applicable:

* Insurance products sold through licensed agents/brokers
* Stop-loss coverage maintained for self-insured plans
* State mandates evaluated and incorporated

---

## Wellness Program Compliance (ACA & EEOC)

### ACA Wellness Rules

* Reasonable alternative standards offered
* Reward limits (up to 30% of coverage cost for outcomes-based programs)
* Notice requirements met

### EEOC/ADA Compliance

* Participation is voluntary
* No disability-based discrimination
* Medical inquiries limited to permissible wellness activities

---

## Tax Reporting

### Employer Obligations

| Form | Purpose | Timing |
|------|---------|--------|
| Form 1095-C | ACA MEC reporting | Annual (January 31) |
| Form W-2 | Employee compensation | Annual (January 31) |
| Form 1099-MISC | Reward payments (if applicable) | Annual (January 31) |

### Participant Obligations

* Participants report taxable rewards as income
* MEC coverage reported on individual tax return (if required)

---

## RWA-Specific Compliance

### Securities Law

* RWAs not offered to participants
* Institutional/accredited investors only
* No public offering

### Banking/Money Transmission

* No fiat custody by RWA layer
* All banking through regulated channels

### ERISA/Plan Asset Rules

* RWAs do not represent plan assets
* Clear separation documented

---

## Ongoing Compliance Monitoring

### Annual Activities

* Non-discrimination testing (§105, §125)
* Form 5500 filing (if required)
* ACA reporting
* Plan document review

### Quarterly Activities

* Claims audit
* Fiduciary review
* Service provider oversight

---

## Summary

The AYG platform is designed to comply with all applicable federal and state regulations. RWA infrastructure is layered separately and does not interfere with regulatory compliance.
