# Risk Disclosures

## Purpose

This document identifies and describes material risks associated with the AYG platform, its operations, capital structure, and RWA integration. It is written for institutional review and investment decision-making.

**Important:** This is not a complete list of risks. Additional risks may emerge as operations evolve and regulatory guidance develops.

---

## Regulatory Risk

### IRS Guidance Evolution

**Risk:** IRS may issue new guidance affecting wellness program rewards or cafeteria plan structures.

**Mitigation:**

* Formal legal opinion obtained
* Structure aligns with current guidance
* Continuous monitoring of IRS announcements
* Flexibility to restructure if needed

**Impact if Realized:**

* Potential reclassification of reward payments
* Increased participant tax liability
* Required program redesign

---

### DOL/ERISA Enforcement

**Risk:** Department of Labor may challenge plan structure or fiduciary arrangements.

**Mitigation:**

* Clear ERISA compliance structure
* Third-party administrator involvement
* Formal plan documents
* Regular fiduciary review

**Impact if Realized:**

* Potential penalties or corrective actions
* Operational disruptions
* Participant notices required

---

### ACA Regulatory Changes

**Risk:** Future ACA modifications could affect MEC requirements or employer mandates.

**Mitigation:**

* MEC component designed to meet core requirements
* Structure adaptable to regulatory changes
* Legal counsel monitors developments

**Impact if Realized:**

* Program redesign required
* Potential increased costs
* Temporary operational disruption

---

### State Insurance Regulation

**Risk:** State regulators may challenge self-insured structure or wellness programs.

**Mitigation:**

* Stop-loss insurance in place
* Compliance with state-specific requirements
* Multi-state legal review

**Impact if Realized:**

* State-specific modifications required
* Potential market restrictions
* Licensing requirements

---

## Tax Risk

### Reward Payment Characterization

**Risk:** IRS audit could challenge tax treatment of reward payments.

**Mitigation:**

* Formal legal opinion supporting structure
* Clear distinction from fixed indemnity
* Proper documentation and substantiation
* Consistent operational practices

**Impact if Realized:**

* Reclassification as taxable income
* Back taxes and penalties for participants
* Employer reporting corrections
* Reputational damage

**Likelihood:** Moderate (IRS guidance exists but novel structures face scrutiny)

---

### Cafeteria Plan Operation

**Risk:** Operational errors in cafeteria plan administration could trigger tax issues.

**Mitigation:**

* Professional third-party administrator
* Annual compliance review
* Clear documentation
* Participant education

**Impact if Realized:**

* Loss of favorable tax treatment
* Participant tax liability
* Corrective distributions required

---

## Operational Risk

### Administrator/TPA Dependency

**Risk:** Platform relies on third-party administrators for critical functions.

**Mitigation:**

* Multiple administrator relationships
* Backup service providers identified
* Clear service level agreements
* Regular performance monitoring

**Impact if Realized:**

* Service disruptions
* Participant complaints
* Regulatory reporting delays
* Potential participant liabilities

---

### Technology & Data Security

**Risk:** Cybersecurity breach could expose participant data.

**Mitigation:**

* Business Associate Agreements
* Regular security audits
* Incident response plan

**Impact if Realized:**

* Regulatory penalties
* Participant harm
* Reputational damage

---

### Employer Adoption & Retention

**Risk:** Platform growth depends on employer adoption and retention.

## RWA-Specific Risk

### Legal/Regulatory Classification Risk

**Risk:** Regulators may view RWAs differently than intended, potentially as:

* Securities requiring registration
* Plan assets under ERISA
* Insurance products requiring licensing
* Money transmission requiring licensing

**Mitigation:**

* Conservative legal structure
* Formal legal opinions
* Institutional/accredited counterparties only
* Clear boundary documentation
* No retail offering

**Impact if Realized:**

* Registration requirements
* Operational restrictions
* Regulatory enforcement
* Program redesign required

---

### Technology & Smart Contract Risk

**Risk:** If RWAs are implemented on blockchain:

* Smart contract bugs or vulnerabilities
* Blockchain network issues
* Key management risks

**Mitigation:**

* Audited smart contracts
* Multi-sig controls
* Established blockchain platforms
* Disaster recovery procedures

**Impact if Realized:**

* Loss of RWA functionality
* Capital partner concerns
* Reporting disruptions
* Need to revert to manual processes

---

### Market Acceptance Risk

**Risk:** Capital partners may not value RWA transparency as expected.

**Mitigation:**

* Demonstrable benefits (lower cost of capital)
* Institutional education
* Gradual adoption
* Traditional reporting alongside RWAs

**Impact if Realized:**

* No capital efficiency gains
* Wasted infrastructure investment
* Continued higher cost of capital
