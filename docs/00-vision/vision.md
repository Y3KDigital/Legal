# Vision

We are building a shared, defensible operating system for a legal + tax + engineering team working on regulated digital-asset products, with a focus on:

- RWA issuance programs
- Securities characterization and offering workflows
- Stablecoin operations and payment rails
- Multi-chain smart contract systems

## Core Principle

Everything is a **state machine with gates**:

- A product flow is a series of states.
- Each transition requires evidence (documents, approvals, filings, controls).
- Evidence is stored, referenced, and reviewable.

## What “Compliant by Design” means here

- Explicit assumptions per jurisdiction
- Control mapping (KYC/AML, sanctions, disclosures, custody, tax)
- Separation of duties (legal vs tax vs engineering)
- Auditability
