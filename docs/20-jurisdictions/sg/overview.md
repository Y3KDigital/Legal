# Singapore (Template)

Status: **DRAFT**

## Scope

Singapore's progressive digital-asset framework under MAS (Monetary Authority of Singapore).

## Key Regulators

- **MAS**: securities, payment services, AML/CFT

## Token Classifications

### Digital Payment Tokens (DPTs)
Tokens used as medium of exchange (not securities or e-money).

**Key Requirements** (under Payment Services Act):
- License required for DPT services (exchange, custody, transfer)
- AML/CFT controls
- Technology risk management
- Safeguarding of customer assets

### Capital Markets Products (Securities Tokens)
Tokens that are securities, units in collective investment schemes, or derivatives.

**Key Requirements** (under Securities and Futures Act):
- Prospectus or exemption
- Capital markets services license for dealing/custody
- Conduct rules

### Stablecoins
MAS treats stablecoins as either:
- DPTs (if not securities or e-money)
- Securities (if investment features)
- E-money (if narrow definition met; very rare)

**Recent Developments**:
- Stablecoin regulatory framework consultation (2022-2023)
- Proposed reserve/redemption requirements for single-currency stablecoins

## AML/KYC
