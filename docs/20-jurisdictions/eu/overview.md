# European Union (Template)

Status: **DRAFT**

## Scope

EU regulatory framework for digital assets, with focus on:
- MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation)
- MiFID II (for securities tokens)
- E-Money Directive / EMD2
- AMLD5/AMLD6 (AML requirements)

## Key Regulators

- **ESMA** (European Securities and Markets Authority): securities tokens, trading platforms
- **EBA** (European Banking Authority): AML, payment tokens
- **National competent authorities**: member-state level licensing/supervision

## Token Classifications

### Asset-Referenced Tokens (ARTs)
Tokens that maintain stable value by reference to multiple fiat currencies, commodities, or crypto-assets.

**Key Requirements** (under MiCA):
- Authorization required if significant
- Reserve requirements
- White paper publication
- Ongoing reporting

### E-Money Tokens (EMTs)
Tokens that maintain stable value by reference to a single fiat currency.

**Key Requirements**:
- E-money institution authorization (or credit institution)
- Full backing in same currency
- Redemption at par
- Segregated reserves

### Utility Tokens
Tokens providing access to goods/services.

**Key Requirements**:
- White paper (if offered to public)
- May avoid financial services regulation if truly utility-only

### Security Tokens
Tokens qualifying as "transferable securities" or other MiFID financial instruments.

**Key Requirements**:
- MiFID II / Prospectus Regulation apply
- Issuance may require prospectus or exemption
- Trading may require MTF/OTF authorization

## AML/KYC

- AMLD5/AMLD6 apply to "crypto-asset service providers"
- Customer due diligence, ongoing monitoring, suspicious transaction reporting
- Travel Rule (TFR) for transfers

## Cross-Border

- MiCA creates EU-wide passport for certain services once licensed in one member state
- For securities, Prospectus Regulation provides EU passport

## Open Questions (DRAFT)

- MiCA full application dates and transitional provisions
- National implementation differences
- Interaction with existing e-money/securities regimes
