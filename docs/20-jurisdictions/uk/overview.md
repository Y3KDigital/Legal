# United Kingdom (Template)

Status: **DRAFT**

## Scope

Post-Brexit UK crypto-asset regime, including:
- FCA crypto-asset rules
- Financial Promotions Order (FPO)
- Proposed stablecoin regime
- Existing securities/e-money laws

## Key Regulators

- **FCA** (Financial Conduct Authority): registration, promotions, AML
- **Bank of England**: stablecoin systemic oversight (proposed)
- **HM Treasury**: policy and legislation

## Token Classifications

### Unregulated Tokens
Exchange tokens (e.g., Bitcoin) are currently **not** regulated investments, but:
- AML registration required for exchange/custody services
- Financial promotions restrictions apply

### Security Tokens
Tokens that meet definition of "Specified Investment" under RAO (Regulated Activities Order).

**Key Requirements**:
- Prospectus (or exemption) for offers
- Authorization required for issuance, trading, custody
- FCA approval process

### E-Money Tokens
Tokens meeting e-money definition under EMRs (Electronic Money Regulations).

**Key Requirements**:
- E-money institution authorization
- Safeguarding/redemption rules

### Stablecoins (Proposed)
HM Treasury/BoE proposing regulation for systemic stablecoins used as means of payment.

**Expected Requirements**:
- Authorization and supervision
- Reserve/backing rules
- Operational resilience
- Wind-down planning

## AML/KYC

- Cryptoasset businesses must register with FCA under Money Laundering Regulations
- Customer due diligence, ongoing monitoring, SARs
- Travel Rule compliance

## Financial Promotions

- Promotions of crypto-assets to UK persons restricted (FPO 2023 amendments)
- Must be authorized firm or approved by authorized firm
- Risk warnings required

## Open Questions (DRAFT)

- Timeline for stablecoin regime implementation
- Custody rule finalization
- DeFi treatment
