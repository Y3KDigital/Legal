# Switzerland (Template)

Status: **DRAFT**

## Scope

Switzerland's crypto-friendly framework with clear rules for tokens and financial services.

## Key Regulators

- **FINMA** (Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority)
- **SRO** (Self-Regulatory Organizations): AML for non-licensed entities

## Token Classifications (FINMA Guidance)

### Payment Tokens
Means of payment; not securities.

**Key Requirements**:
- AML requirements (via SRO or FINMA)
- No securities prospectus

### Utility Tokens
Access to digital services.

**Key Requirements**:
- No FINMA license if purely utility
- AML if traded on exchange

### Asset Tokens (Security Tokens)
Represent assets, debt, equity, or similar rights.

**Key Requirements**:
- Securities laws apply
- Prospectus or exemption
- Banking/securities dealer license may be required

## DLT Act (2021)

- Legal framework for DLT securities, trading facilities, and bankruptcy segregation
- "DLT trading facility" license
- Segregated DLT custody

## AML/KYC

- Anti-Money Laundering Act applies
- CDD, monitoring, reporting to MROS
- SRO membership required for many crypto businesses

## Banking License Exemptions

- Fintech license (up to CHF 100m deposits, no interest)
- No license if client funds held < 60 days (ICO exemption)
