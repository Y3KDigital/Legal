# United Arab Emirates (Template)

Status: **DRAFT**

## Scope

UAE has multiple regulatory regimes due to mainland vs free zones (DIFC, ADGM).

## Key Regulators

- **SCA** (Securities and Commodities Authority): mainland securities
- **CBUAE** (Central Bank of UAE): payment tokens, stablecoins (proposed)
- **DFSA** (Dubai Financial Services Authority): DIFC free zone
- **FSRA** (Financial Services Regulatory Authority): ADGM free zone
- **VARA** (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority): Dubai mainland virtual assets

## Token Classifications

### VARA Jurisdiction (Dubai Mainland)
- Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) require licensing
- AML/CFT requirements
- Marketing and custody rules

### DIFC (DFSA)
- Crypto Token framework (2022)
- Investment Tokens (securities)
- Utility Tokens
- Licensing for exchanges, custody, advisory

### ADGM (FSRA)
- Virtual Asset Framework (2020, updated)
- Regulated/Recognized Virtual Assets
- Licensing for custody, exchange, advisory

### CBUAE (Proposed)
- Payment Token Services regulation (consultation stage)
- Stablecoin reserve requirements expected

## AML/KYC

- Each regime has AML/CFT requirements
- CDD, monitoring, STR filing
- Cabinet Decision 10/2019 on AML/CFT

## Open Questions (DRAFT)

- CBUAE payment token framework final rules
- Coordination between mainland and free zone regimes
- Cross-border service provision
